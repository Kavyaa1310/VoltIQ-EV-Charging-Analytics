### VoltIQ – EV Charging Analytics



**Project Overview**



* VoltIQ – EV Charging Analytics is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and monitor electric vehicle (EV) charging network performance.
* The project provides insights into charging sessions, energy consumption, revenue generation, customer behaviour, charging types, stations, cities, payment methods, and vehicle types.
* The dashboard is designed to help businesses understand charging network performance and identify important trends using interactive visualizations and filters.





**Objectives**



* &#x20;Analyze total EV charging sessions.
* &#x20;Monitor energy consumption across charging stations.
* &#x20;Analyze total and average revenue.
* &#x20;Understand customer and charging behaviour.
* &#x20;Compare station performance.
* &#x20;Analyze revenue by city and payment method.
* &#x20;Compare different charging types.
* &#x20;Analyze revenue by vehicle type.
* &#x20;Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.





**Tools \& Technologies**



**Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development

**Microsoft Excel** – Dataset storage and data preparation

**Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation

**DAX** – Measures and calculations
